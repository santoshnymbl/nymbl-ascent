Nymbl Ascent

A game-based behavioral assessment platform that reveals who someone is — not just what they've memorized.

Psychology-grounded. Deterministic scoring. Hard to game.

The Problem

Traditional screening is broken

📄

Resumes lie

Credentials don't predict performance. You're screening for polish, not potential.

🎭

Interviews are biased

Charisma and rehearsed answers win over genuine competence and values alignment.

📝

Skills tests miss what matters

Coding puzzles and case studies test knowledge, not judgment under ambiguity.

How It Works

Three stages. 15 minutes. Zero guesswork.

Stage 1

Reveal

Four micro-games that surface implicit preferences through clicks, not words. No right answers — just choices that reveal priorities.

Stage 2

Build

Branching workplace scenarios with depleting resources. Every choice has a cost — there's no way to game the "right" answer.

Stage 3

Grow

A role-specific challenge auto-generated from the job description. Engineers debug code. Consultants handle escalations. Sales reps handle objections.

Hard to Game

Hidden signals. Honest results.

⏱️

Decision Pace

Thoughtful responses (2–10s) score higher than instant clicks or excessive deliberation.

🔄

Revision Tracking

Changing your mind shows reflection — a positive signal, not a penalty.

🎯

Cross-Stage Consistency

Values expressed in Stage 1 should align with decisions in Stage 2. Inconsistency flags gaming.

🛡️

Pattern Detection

Random clicking, always-first-option, and speed-running are detected and penalized.
